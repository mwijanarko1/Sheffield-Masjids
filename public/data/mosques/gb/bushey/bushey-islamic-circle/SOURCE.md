# Data source

Bushey Islamic Circle (`bushey/bushey-islamic-circle`), The Bushey Centre, Bushey Country Club, Bushey High Street, WD23 1TT.

- HTML timetable at `https://www.busheyislamiccircle.org.uk/timetable/`. It has "Next few days", "November" and "December" tabs, and each row gives begins and jama'ah for Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha in 12h format.
- Friday Zuhr jama'ah is the Jumu'ah time, and it varies by week. Site text: "Winter timings: Adhan 12:45pm, Khutbah 1:15pm".
- Coordinates are the WD23 1TT postcode centroid (postcodes.io), not a surveyed point.
